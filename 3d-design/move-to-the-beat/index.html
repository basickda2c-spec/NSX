Funko-Style Dancing Character

This stylized Funko-inspired 3D character was modeled and rendered in Blender, focusing on clean topology and proportions characteristic of the collectible vinyl style. The character was rigged and animated using Mixamo, leveraging motion-capture data to achieve smooth and dynamic dance movements. Materials and shading were optimized for a stylized aesthetic while maintaining efficient geometry suitable for both animation and real-time applications. Lighting and rendering in Blender were carefully set up to highlight the character’s form and playful motion, resulting in a polished, professional showcase of both modeling and animation workflow.
